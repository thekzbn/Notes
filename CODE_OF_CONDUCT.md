# Code of Conduct

This repository is for sharing and improving class and self-study notes. I want discussion about the notes to stay useful and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful when discussing notes or suggesting changes.
- Critique the content, not the person who wrote it.
- Explain corrections and suggestions clearly.
- When a note is unclear or incomplete, ask for clarification instead of presenting a guess as fact.
- Respect the contribution rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), including the exclusion of OD documents.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or threatening language.
- Deliberately misleading edits or changes that misrepresent the source of a note.
- Repeatedly ignoring the repository’s contribution rules.

## Reporting a concern

If someone’s behaviour makes it difficult to participate, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriate.
